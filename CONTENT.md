# Content

The books bundled with SpeakAlong (texts, Spanish translations and line timings in `public/content/`,
audio streamed from the media server, and covers in `public/covers/`) are included so the app keeps
working for learners now that the service is free.

- They are provided for **personal, educational and non-commercial use**.
- Some books are adaptations of works whose rights belong to their respective authors and publishers.
  Those rights remain with their owners; the MIT License of this repository covers the source code only.
- If you hold rights over any of this material and want it removed, please
  [open an issue](https://github.com/r4yg/speakalong/issues) and it will be taken down.
